Privacy Policy

Apps: Guitar Trainer: Chord Tone / Guitar Trainer: Scale

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Guitar Trainer: Chord Tone and Guitar Trainer: Scale.

Accounts

You do not need to create an account with the app developer to use either app. In-app purchases use Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The apps store songs you save, including song names, descriptions, and chord progressions. They also store app settings and a local indicator of whether Pro access is active. This information is stored in the apps' storage on your device.

Depending on your Android and Google account settings, Android backup may include app data.

Pro purchases

Both apps offer optional Pro access through an in-app purchase processed by Google Play. The apps request product and purchase information from Google Play and use purchase status and a purchase token to enable or restore Pro access and complete purchase processing.

The apps do not save purchase tokens with your Saved Songs or app settings. Google Play handles payment information; the apps do not request or receive your full payment card number.

Data retention and deletion

Saved Songs and settings remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app's local data. You can delete individual Saved Songs within the app. You can remove local app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Android backups and Google Play purchase records are managed separately by Google under your account settings and Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the apps or their data practices change. The updated policy and its revision date will be published on this page.
